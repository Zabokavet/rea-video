# READY PROMPTS — 20 "Rules" Narration Videos
Modeled on the Prycken Jasper / Apex Predator format (analyzed 2026-11-18). All scripts are original. They use the same genre and structure, and no lines are copied.

## HOW TO USE
1. Paste the **MASTER BLOCK** into your video generator first. It sets the voice, visuals, text and structure.
2. Paste **one** VIDEO block under it. One paste makes one video.
3. Post one video per day. Every script ends with a follow CTA. To sell one of your books at the end instead, name the book and the ending of all 20 scripts will be swapped.

Order of operations, fixed in the prompt: **book open → pen underlines sentence 1 while the voice reads it → sentence 2 → … → page turn → closing lines.**

## MASTER BLOCK (paste this first, every time)

INSTRUCTION TO THE AI MAKING THIS VIDEO: Use every skill, tool and capability installed in you to deliver ONE finished video, ready to post as-is — picture, voice, music, sync and timing all complete. Do not return raw clips, silent footage or unfinished pieces. If something can be done better with a tool you have, use it.

Create a vertical 9:16 faceless video. TOTAL LENGTH: 55–75 seconds — let the full script be read at a natural pace; never cut or speed up lines to hit a time. THE VISUAL IS THE FORMAT, follow it exactly: a close-up of an open book lying on a dark wooden desk in a dim, moody room, warm lamp light falling across the pages from the side. The book's cover is never shown, only the open pages. The sentences of the script are printed in the book in a clean, large serif font, easy to read on a phone screen. A man's hand holding a black pen moves across the page and underlines each sentence, one at a time, in the exact order they are spoken — the pen is always on the same sentence the narrator is reading. The camera looks down at the book at a slight angle and pushes in very slowly. No faces are ever shown, only the book, the hand and the pen. When a page is finished, the hand turns the page and the underlining continues on the next one. THE VOICE — GENERATE IT AS PART OF THE VIDEO: a calm, deep, authoritative male AI voice reads every sentence in the book out loud, dry and forward. The voice must be crystal clear and easy to understand: every word pronounced fully and distinctly, a steady natural pace, never rushed, never mumbling. No other voices, no introduction — the first line starts immediately, because the first line is the hook. Then the rules come one after another, no long pauses, no explanations, until the closing lines at the end. THE MATCHING — THIS MUST BE PERFECT: the voice, the pen and the printed sentence stay locked together the whole video. The pen underlines a sentence only while the voice is reading that exact sentence — never ahead, never behind. When the voice moves to the next sentence, the pen moves with it. THE MUSIC: one low, dark background music bed under the voice for the whole video, never louder than the voice. No logos, no watermarks, no text anywhere except the sentences printed in the book. Before finishing, check the video yourself: length in range, voice clear on every word, pen and voice matched on every line. Fix anything that fails before delivering it.
